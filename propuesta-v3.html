Financing

Programs for residents and non-residents

If you're interested in obtaining a mortgage, we can help advise you on which program could work for you. There are various programs available for both residents and non-residents. The mortgage company will handle the entire process. If you decide to pay off your mortgage before the agreed-upon time, there are no penalties.

01

FHA Loan

The government supports this loan, which is easy to qualify for with low initial and interest rates.

02

Conventional 30-Year Fixed

These loans offer very low fixed interest rates for up to 30 years.

03

Conventional 15-Year Fixed

Low rates, designed to pay less interest over a 15-year fixed term.

04

VA Loan

Exclusively available to active military and veterans for buying or refinancing their homes, with zero or very low interest rates.

05

Reverse Mortgage

This financial tool eliminates mortgage payments and provides cash for homeowners over 62.

06

Foreign Loans

Offered to non-U.S. residents who wish to purchase properties with down payments starting at 25–35% and low interest rates.
